Partners & Complementary Services

To provide a comprehensive experience for food truck operators and customers, the Food Truck Nerdz (FTN) app can integrate with various third-party services. These partnerships enhance operational efficiency, marketing reach, and customer satisfaction.

Point of Sale (POS) Systems

POS integrations are critical for synchronizing menu items, inventory, and sales data directly with the FTN tracker.

Lavu

Website

lavu.com

Features

Mobile hardware bundles (3G/4G), online ordering, inventory management ("86" countdown), and Marty AI for analytics.

Benefits

Specifically designed for mobile environments like food trucks; reliable cloud connectivity and robust reporting.

Square

Website

squareup.com

Features

Easy setup, transparent pricing, and extensive third-party app marketplace.

Benefits

Highly portable hardware and a massive existing user base among small vendors.

Toast

Website

toasttab.com

Features

Built-in loyalty programs, online ordering, and delivery integration.

Benefits

Highly optimized for restaurant workflows and high-volume service.

Clover

Website

clover.com

Features

Compact hardware (Clover Flex/Mini), LTE connectivity, and a robust third-party app market.

Benefits

Highly portable for outside service and easily scales with additional software modules.

Online Ordering & Delivery

Expanding reach beyond the truck’s physical location through delivery platforms.

  • DoorDash / Uber Eats / Grubhub: Direct integration allows orders to flow into the truck’s POS.

  • ShipDay: Commission-free delivery management for trucks using their own drivers.

Location & Fleet Management

Ensuring accurate tracking and efficient movement of trucks.

  • Roaming Hunger: A popular platform for booking and discovery that can complement FTN’s tracking.

  • Route4Me: Optimizer for fleet routing if managing multiple trucks.

Marketing & Customer Loyalty

Engaging with the community and rewarding frequent flyers.

  • Stamp Me / Yollty: Digital loyalty card solutions that work via mobile wallets.

  • Instagram / TikTok: Essential for real-time updates; FTN can hook into these APIs to pull live content.

  • Braze / Klaviyo: Lifecycle marketing tools for sending geo-fenced push notifications or emails.

Event Organizers & Local Businesses

Partnerships for consistent placement and high traffic.

  • Local Breweries & Taprooms: Mutually beneficial partnerships where trucks provide food for drink-only establishments.

  • Corporate Campuses: Scheduled rotations through services like Roaming Hunger or Best Food Trucks.
